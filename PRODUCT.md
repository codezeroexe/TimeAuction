# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Students and participants at a live competition event (up to about 35 stations, shared LAN; matches of 2 to 12 players), playing alone or in teams of up to 5, usually on a station PC in a bright hall. Event administrators and judges run scoring and the leaderboard from `#/admin`.

## Product Purpose
TimeAuction — Engineering Across Time. Participants register, receive 1,000 ChronoCoins, bid in a live auction for breakthrough technologies (5 to 20 lots, drawn from 50 per match), face one of 30 Timeline Twists, receive a Future Mission (Mars Settlement), then engineer and lock a solution from two or three acquired technologies. Judges score six criteria (100 points); results are verified, published and ranked on a leaderboard. Success: smooth, fair, legible events.

## Positioning
Auction-and-engineering game with a hash-chained event log, server-authoritative bids, and single-device fallback.

## Constraints
- Content, flows and routes unchanged; redesign is visual only.
- Must stay legible on projectors and at distance; keyboard and reduced-motion support kept.
- Brand commitment (user, 2026-10-13): cyberpunk / Matrix aesthetic, matrix-green led, dark default with a light fallback.
